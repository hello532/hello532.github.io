How this site is funded

This site publishes guides for store owners about outsourcing launch work, and links to freelance marketplace categories where that work can be bought. It intends to earn affiliate commission on qualifying purchases made through those links.

Current status, stated plainly. Two marketplaces, two different states, and no tracked link on the site yet.

Fiverr. An application to the Fiverr Affiliate Program was filed on 4 September 2026 and is under review. Until it is approved, every Fiverr link here is an ordinary link carrying no affiliate tracking. The links are already marked up as sponsored so search engines and anyone reading the page source can see the commercial intent before any tracking exists. If the application is approved, the links get replaced with tracked ones and this paragraph is updated the same day.

Upwork. An affiliate account exists, created on 4 September 2026 through Impact, the platform Upwork runs its affiliate program on. No tracked Upwork link has been placed on this site and none will be until three questions are answered in writing by Upwork. First, their affiliate terms let them withhold payout for "any referral or traffic to Upwork Sites from search engines", and this site is read almost entirely through organic search. Second, the same terms require prior written permission before posting "other content concerning the Upwork Offerings", which on a literal reading covers describing Upwork factually at all. Third, they prohibit promotional methods defined in a Master Campaign Agreement that has not been supplied, so the restricted list cannot be read. Until those are answered, Upwork is mentioned here as plain text with untracked links only. Their terms also pay out only on a first purchase by someone located in the United States, so for most readers of this site an Upwork referral would earn nothing even if it were tracked.

Nothing on this page is written to imply a partnership that does not yet exist.

When affiliate links are live, the arrangement works like this: if you click through and make a qualifying purchase, this site may receive a commission from the marketplace. Your price does not change. Commission is paid out of the marketplace's revenue, not added to your order.

What this site will not do

  • No invented prices. Freelance rates change constantly and vary by seller, scope, and turnaround. Rather than publish numbers that go stale, the guides explain what moves a quote and link to live listings so you read current prices yourself.
  • No seller endorsements. Nobody here has purchased from every seller in a category. The guides teach vetting criteria instead of naming specific sellers as guaranteed choices.
  • No fabricated results, screenshots, or testimonials. No income claims, no invented case studies, no "clients" that do not exist.
  • No earnings promises. Outsourcing well reduces rework. It does not guarantee that a store sells anything.
  • No pressure tactics. No countdown timers, fake scarcity, unauthorised discount claims, or coupon codes that were not officially issued.
  • No unsolicited email. No purchased lists, no scraped contacts.
